---
title: Lens Assistant
description: A tool-calling chat assistant backed by the MCP tool catalog.
---

Lens Assistant is the chat workspace for asking Lens to inspect clusters, deployments, and other platform resources. The page lets you pick an AI backend, send messages, and review the assistant's tool activity turn by turn.

## Start a conversation with an internal deployment

1. Open **Lens Assistant**.
2. In the right-side **AI provider** panel, keep **Internal** selected.
3. Under **1. Cluster**, open **Select a Cluster…** and choose a ready cluster.
4. Under **2. Deployment (model service)**, open **Select a Deployment…** and choose a ready deployment.
5. Click into the message box when its placeholder changes to **Message Lens…**.
6. Type your question and press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send it.
7. Confirm the assistant is connected by checking the status card that starts with **Talking to**.

## Start a conversation with an external provider

1. In **AI provider**, click **External**.
2. Under **External AI provider**, open **Select an AI provider…** and choose a configured provider.
3. Click into the message box when its placeholder shows **Message Lens…**.
4. Type your question and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

## Start over with a new chat

1. Click **New chat** in the page header.
2. If needed, reselect **Internal** or **External**, then choose the service you want to use.
3. Send the first message in the cleared conversation.

## Review the assistant's reasoning and tool activity

1. After you send a message, expand **Thinking** to review the assistant's reasoning summary.
2. Expand **Tool call** entries to inspect the tool name, arguments, and result.
3. If the assistant shows **Permission required**, stop and use a page where your account has the required access.
4. If a tool is unavailable, Lens shows **Blocked (unknown tool)** in the tool timeline.

## Approve or reject a requested action

1. When a turn pauses on **Needs approval** or **Needs confirmation**, expand that entry.
2. Review the request details and arguments.
3. If the entry includes the checkbox **I reviewed the arguments above and want to proceed.**, select it.
4. Click **Approve** to continue or **Reject** to stop that action.
5. Watch the status change to **Approving…**, **Rejecting…**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**.

## Send a voice message

1. If your browser supports speech input, choose a language from **Voice input language**. Language names are displayed in English (for example, **Chinese**).
2. Click the microphone button with the tooltip **Speak your question (runs locally in your browser)**.
3. Speak your request.
4. Click the microphone again when its tooltip changes to **Stop recording**.
5. Wait for **Transcribing your voice message…**, then let Lens send the transcribed message.

## Requirements

For the **External** mode, Lens needs a provider that appears in **Select an AI provider…**. If the panel shows **No External AI providers configured yet.**, an administrator must add one under [External providers](/user-guide/external-providers).

## Connect an external MCP client

Lens Assistant uses Lens' built-in MCP tool catalog. To drive the same tools from your own
MCP client (MCP Inspector, an IDE agent, …), see [MCP integration](/mcp).
